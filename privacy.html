UO Architect

House & Deco Planner · Privacy statement

UO Architect does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

What stays on your computer

  • Your house designs and settings are saved on your own device (in the app's local storage). Nothing is uploaded.
  • If you choose to read your own Ultima Online folder, the app reads only the game files it needs to draw pieces and play music, and builds a private copy on your device. Those files are never sent anywhere and are not included in the app.
  • Exported design files contain only item numbers and positions, never game art.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no cookies sent to us.
  • The desktop app makes no network connections.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/uoarchitect/uoarchitect.github.io.
