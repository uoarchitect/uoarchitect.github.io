How to use UO Architect
  • Place: pick a piece in the catalog, then click (or drag) on the plot.
  • (S) Select / move: click a piece to select it, drag to move it.
  • (E) Erase: or Del removes a selection.
  • (R) Rotates: the piece you are holding or have selected.
  • (H) Hue: Opens the hue field.
  • (↑ / ↓) Raise or lower the selected piece.
  • Shift + drag pans the view. Holding Shift while placing keeps same-on-same pieces from replacing each other.
  • Floors 1–4: Click the active floor twice to see through the walls.
  • Ctrl+Z undoes 1 step. Ctrl+Y redoes it.
  • Save / Load keep five slots in this browser.
  • Export gives a JSON file you can share – it holds item numbers only, never game art.
  • (Foundation check): uncheck to edit foundation.

Navigate to where client.exe is located
C:\Program Files (x86)\Electronic Arts\Ultima Online Classic\
That is the usual place. If yours is elsewhere, pick the folder that contains client.exe. In the window that opens, click the address bar, paste the path, press Enter, then click Select Folder.

This page contains no Ultima Online artwork. To see the pieces, point it at your own UO folder (the one with artLegacyMUL.uop and tiledata.mul in it).

  • The art and the game music are built on your computer, in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  • It is remembered here, so you only do this once.
  • Saved and exported designs hold only item numbers, never the art.
